## What you'll accomplish

- View your current plan and usage
- Update payment method or billing details
- Know where to compare plans before upgrading

## Manage billing

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Billing settings

    From the sidebar user menu, go to **Settings → Billing**. Owners see plan details, invoices, and payment methods.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Compare plans

    Visit the [Pricing page](/pricing) for feature comparisons across Free and paid tiers.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Upgrade or change plan

    Choose a new tier from Billing settings. Changes apply according to your subscription terms shown at checkout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What typically scales by plan

| Capability | Free | Paid tiers |
| ---------- | ---- | ---------- |
| Workspaces | Limited | Higher limits |
| Team members | Limited | Team growth |
| Priority support | Community | Email / priority |

<Callout type="info">
  Exact limits and prices are shown on the live [Pricing](/pricing) page — they may change as AnexaPress evolves.
</Callout>
