## What you can do here

Whether you are a sustainability lead or a distributed operations team, AnexaPress gives you one place to:

- **Organize emissions data** in workspace-scoped records
- **Collaborate with teammates** using roles and permissions
- **Prepare reporting workflows** without scattered spreadsheets
- **Manage access securely** across departments and contractors

## How teams typically use AnexaPress

1. **Operations** records activity data and keeps source documents organized.
2. **Sustainability / ESG** reviews entries and prepares summaries for reporting cycles.
3. **Leadership** uses workspace settings to manage team access and oversight.

The guides in this documentation follow that real workflow — not abstract feature lists.
