## What you can do

- Create a site for your team
- Write pages and blog posts in a visual editor
- Configure header, footer, and site appearance
- Publish content and view it at a public URL
- Upload images and files to the media library

## Who this is for

- **Editors** or **publishers** in a workspace
- Teams running a **company site** or **blog**
- Anyone who needs to update copy without a developer

<Callout type="info">
  CMS access depends on your role. Editors can create drafts; publishers and admins can publish to the live site. If a button is missing, ask the workspace owner to assign the **Editor** or **Publisher** role.
</Callout>

## Where to find the CMS

1. Open your workspace and the sidebar.
2. Go to **Sites**.
3. Select a site — you'll see overview, pages, blog, settings, and files.

Your public site URL looks like:

`https://platform.your-domain.com/{workspace-slug}/{site-slug}`

Example: `/acme-corp/main` — home page of site `main` in workspace `acme-corp`.

## If something isn't working

- **I don't see Sites** — you may lack workspace access or have the **Viewer** role only.
- **Publish is disabled** — you need publisher permissions; save a draft and ask a colleague with the Publisher role.
- **Site won't open** — make sure the page or post is **published**, not still a draft.
- **Image won't upload** — check format (JPG, PNG, WebP) and file size; refresh the page if it fails again.

<Callout type="warning">
  If the issue persists — contact the workspace owner or support via the **Help** button on the site.
</Callout>
