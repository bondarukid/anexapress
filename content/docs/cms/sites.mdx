## What you can do

- Create a new site in your workspace (when multi-site mode is enabled)
- Set name, URL slug, and default SEO
- Configure site header navigation and footer
- Connect a custom domain

## Who this is for

- Workspace owners and admins
- Anyone configuring the **look** of the public site

***

## Step 1: Open the site list

1. In the workspace sidebar, click **Sites**.
2. You'll see site cards with name and last updated date.

> 📌 On first login there's usually a default site named **main**.

***

## Step 2: Create a site (if available)

1. Click **New site** in the top right.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** — how the site appears in the panel
   - **Slug** — URL segment after the workspace (Latin characters, no spaces)
3. Click **Create**.

You'll land on the **site overview**.

***

## Step 3: Configure appearance (Layout)

1. In the site menu, choose **Layout**.
2. Configure the **header**:
   - logo (from media library)
   - menu items — label and page slug for each link
3. Configure the **footer** — copyright text and extra links.
4. Click **Save**.

A **preview** on the right shows how the header and footer will look on the public site.

***

## Step 4: General site settings

Under **Settings**:

| Field | Purpose |
| ----- | ------- |
| Name | Shown in the panel and may appear in titles |
| Slug | Site address: `/{workspace}/{slug}` |
| Default SEO | Title and description when a page has none |
| Domains | Connect your own domain |

***

## What happens next

- The site appears in **Sites**.
- The public URL works once you publish at least the home page.
- Layout changes apply to all pages on the site immediately.

***

## If something isn't working

- **No New site button** — single-site mode is on; use the default site.
- **Menu link returns 404** — check that the page with that slug is **published**.
- **Domain won't connect** — DNS must point to the platform host; allow up to 24 hours after DNS changes.
