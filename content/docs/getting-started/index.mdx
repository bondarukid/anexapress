## What you'll accomplish

- Create your AnexaPress account
- Name your workspace and choose a team URL
- Optionally invite a teammate

<DocsChecklist
  title="What you need before starting"
  items={[
    "An email address you can verify (or a Google account for OAuth sign-in)",
    "A workspace name in mind — usually your company or department",
  ]}
/>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create your account

    Open the [homepage](/) and choose **Start Free**.

    You can sign up with email and password or continue with Google. After verification, AnexaPress walks you through onboarding — create a new workspace or join one with an invite code.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create your workspace

    Pick a **workspace name** (shown to your team) and a **slug** (used in your team URL).

    Think of the workspace as your team's home base: members, roles, and records all live here. You can belong to multiple workspaces and switch between them from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Invite a teammate (optional)

    Open **Team** in the sidebar and send an email invite — or share your **join code** for faster onboarding.

    You can always add people later; see [Invite teammates](/workspaces/invite-team).
  </Step>
</Steps>
