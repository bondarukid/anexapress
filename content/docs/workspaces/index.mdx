  A workspace is where your team and emissions records live together. Everything you do in BondaStefLab SaaS belongs to one workspace at a time.
</GuideHero>

## Why workspaces matter

Most organizations manage more than one site, business unit, or client. Workspaces keep each team's data separate:

- **Members** only see workspaces they belong to
- **Roles and permissions** apply within a workspace
- **Settings** such as timezone and branding are scoped per workspace

You can switch workspaces anytime from the **team switcher** at the top of the sidebar.

## Workspace settings

Open **Workspace settings → General** to configure:

| Setting | Why it helps |
| ------- | ------------ |
| **Display name** | Shown to teammates in the switcher and invites |
| **Slug** | Your team's unique URL — share it carefully; it identifies your workspace |
| **Logo** | Appears in the sidebar switcher for quick recognition |
| **Timezone** | Aligns timestamps and scheduled activity with your team |

## Multiple workspaces

Users often belong to several workspaces — for example, their company plus a client account. The switcher lists every workspace you have joined, ordered by when you joined.

<Callout type="info">
  Switching workspaces changes which records and members you see. Your personal account settings (email, password) stay the same across workspaces.
</Callout>

## Common workflows

- **Agency model:** one workspace per client, invite client stakeholders as Members
- **Enterprise model:** one workspace per region or business unit
- **Solo operator:** one workspace is enough — add a second when you spin up another entity
