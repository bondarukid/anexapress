## Method 1 — Email invite

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Team management

    In the sidebar, click **Team**. You will see current members and pending invites.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Send an invite

    Enter your colleague's email, choose a **role** (Member or Admin), and send.

    They receive an email with a secure link. After signing in (or creating an account), they land directly in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Track pending invites

    Pending invites appear in the team list until accepted or revoked. Revoke any invite that was sent by mistake.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Method 2 — Join code

Join codes are ideal when onboarding several people at once — for example after a reporting cycle or audit sprint.

1. Open **Team** and copy the **join code** (8 characters).
2. Share it privately with teammates.
3. New users enter the code during signup or from an invite link.

<Callout type="warn">
  Treat join codes like office keys — anyone with the code can request access to your workspace. Rotate or disable the code if it leaks.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | What to try |
| ----- | ----------- |
| Invite email not received | Check spam; confirm the email address; resend from **Team** |
| "Already a member" | They may already belong — ask them to switch workspace from the sidebar |
| Wrong workspace after signup | Ensure they used *your* invite link or join code, not a generic signup URL |
