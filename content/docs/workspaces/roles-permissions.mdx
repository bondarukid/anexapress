## Default roles

| Role | Best for | Typical access |
| ---- | -------- | -------------- |
| **Owner** | Founder or billing contact | Everything, including ownership transfer and billing |
| **Admin** | Team lead or ops manager | Members, workspace settings |
| **Member** | Operators, analysts, reviewers | Day-to-day work within assigned access |

<Callout type="info">
  There is exactly one **Owner** per workspace at a time. Ownership can be transferred to another active member when needed.
</Callout>

## Custom permissions

Sometimes a Member needs a narrow extra capability — for example, updating workspace settings without becoming a full Admin. Workspace admins can override permissions per member.

**Good candidates for custom access:**

- A contractor who only reviews submitted records
- An analyst who exports summaries but should not invite users
- A regional lead who manages members in one workspace only

**Avoid over-sharing:** start with Member and add permissions only when someone is blocked.

## Ownership transfer

Transfer ownership when the founder leaves, the company restructures, or billing responsibility moves.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm the recipient

    The new owner must already be an **active member** of the workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Start transfer from Team settings

    The current owner initiates transfer from **Team**. The recipient must confirm acceptance.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Verify billing

    After transfer, billing emails and plan changes follow the new owner. Review [Billing](/billing) if you are on a paid plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Ownership transfer cannot be undone automatically. Double-check the recipient before confirming.
</Callout>
