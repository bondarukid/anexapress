<GuideHero badge="Stay in the loop">
  BondaStefLab SaaS notifies you about invites, team changes, and workspace activity — without flooding your personal inbox unless you choose otherwise.
</GuideHero>

## What you'll accomplish

- Find the notification bell in the dashboard header
- Open the mail center for a full history
- Configure notification preferences

## In-app notifications

The **bell icon** in the top header shows unread activity. Click it to jump to **Mail** — your workspace notification center.

Typical notifications include:

- Workspace invites accepted or declined
- Team membership changes

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Mail

    Click the bell or choose **Mail** from the sidebar (when available in your workspace layout).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review and mark read

    Scan recent items and open anything that needs action — for example, approving a teammate or reviewing a team change.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Adjust preferences

    Open **Settings → Notifications** under your personal account to toggle categories you care about.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Notification preferences are personal — they do not change what your teammates receive.
</Callout>
