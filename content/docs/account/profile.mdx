## Open personal settings

Click your avatar in the sidebar footer → **Account settings**, or navigate to **Settings** from the user menu.

Available sections:

| Section | Purpose |
| ------- | ------- |
| **Personal info** | Name and profile details shown to teammates |
| **Timezone** | Display times in your local zone |
| **Email & password** | Change login credentials |
| **Connected accounts** | Link or unlink Google OAuth |
| **Social URLs** | Optional links on your profile |
| **Danger zone** | Delete account — irreversible |

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Set your timezone

    Choose the timezone that matches where *you* work. Scheduled notifications and timestamps use this preference.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Secure your login

    Use a strong unique password or rely on Google OAuth. Enable recovery options before you need them.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Review connected accounts

    If you linked Google, you can sign in with one click. Unlink only if you have a password set first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting your account removes your access to all workspaces. Workspace data owned by teams you leave may remain — confirm with workspace Owners before deleting.
</Callout>
